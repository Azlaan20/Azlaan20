## About

Electrical Engineer pursuing an **M.Sc. in Electrical Engineering at the University of Stuttgart**, working at the intersection of **machine learning, signal processing, and intelligent engineering systems**.

My background combines industrial electrical engineering at **Engro Polymer & Chemicals**, biomedical-data research at **A*STAR Bioinformatics Institute, Singapore**, and technical projects across computer vision, biomedical signal processing, embedded systems, control, and automation.

I am particularly interested in applying data-driven methods and software to real-world physical and industrial systems.
